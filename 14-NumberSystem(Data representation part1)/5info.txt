what are digital data units
Digital data units are the standard measurements used to quantify the amount
of digital information or computer storage capacity.
Every piece of data in Node.js—whether it is a small text string,
a large video file, or an allocated memory Buffer—is ultimately measured using
these standardized units.🧱The Core Units (The Building Blocks)Bit (b): The smallest possible unit of data.
It holds a single binary value, either 0 or 1.Byte (B): A group of 8 bits.
A single byte is the minimum amount of memory needed to store a single alphanumeric character (like the letter "A") in basic text encoding.
